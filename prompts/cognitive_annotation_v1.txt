Skill definitions:
- attention: Select and maintain focus on relevant details, constraints,
  symbols, options, or visual elements while ignoring distractions.
- working_memory: Hold and manipulate multiple pieces of information or
  intermediate results. Simple recall alone is not working memory.

  assumptions, detect ambiguity, or select and justify a strategy. Routine

  approach. Most routine questions should receive 0 unless self-monitoring is
  genuinely required by the task.
